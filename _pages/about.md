**Research Interests**

我的研究方向聚焦于大语言模型的基础研究，主要关注：

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**  
  Exploring mitigation techniques for factual inconsistencies in generated outputs through in-context sharpness analysis
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**  
  Investigating the universal truthfulness hyperplane within language model outputs
